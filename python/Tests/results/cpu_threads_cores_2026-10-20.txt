Threads and cores in --benchmark, and a portable CPU topology (2026-10-20)
==========================================================================

Build: portable bundle (python/Tests/build_portable_bench_gpu.sh) of the tree
that became the commits after 7cd7231; its banner still says g7cd7231.
Model: tests/gpuprof cases/tl_skin_sub1.xml (5.03 M cells), as in §5.7-5.9.
Hosts: cytex (i9-13900K, RX 6800, UHD 770), srv002 (i7-6700K), srv003
(Ryzen 5 5600G). One simulation at a time per host. Barrier spin 0 (default).
